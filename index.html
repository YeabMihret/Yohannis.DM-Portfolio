Services

3D Modeling (SolidWorks, Fusion 360)

This service involves creating detailed three-dimensional representations of products or components using advanced CAD software like SolidWorks and Fusion 360. These models can be used for visualization, analysis, and manufacturing, allowing for precise design iterations and modifications.

Product Design Industrial Design

This encompasses the entire process of conceptualizing and developing new products. It includes understanding user needs, market trends, and functional requirements to create aesthetically pleasing and functional designs that enhance user experience and fulfill manufacturing constraints.

CAD Drawing Technical Documentation

This service includes the creation of detailed CAD drawings and technical documentation that provide clear specifications for manufacturing and assembly. These documents are essential for ensuring accurate production, compliance with industry standards, and effective communication between design and engineering teams.

Mechanical Simulation (ANSYS, FEA)

Using advanced simulation tools like ANSYS and Finite Element Analysis (FEA), this service helps in predicting how a product will perform under various conditions. By simulating physical phenomena such as stress, heat transfer, and fluid dynamics, potential issues can be identified and addressed early in the design process.

Photorealistic Rendering Animations

This involves creating high-quality visual representations of products through photorealistic rendering techniques. The service can also include animations that showcase how a product works or its features, making it an invaluable tool for marketing, presentations, and client approvals.

Machine Design Custom Part Development

This service involves designing specialized machinery or custom parts tailored to specific applications or client requirements. It includes everything from conceptual design to detailed engineering, ensuring that the final product meets performance criteria and integrates seamlessly into existing systems.
